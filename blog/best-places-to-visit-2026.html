25 Best Places to Visit
in 2026

From ancient temples to tropical islands, these are the world's most breathtaking destinations you need on your 2026 travel bucket list.

Whether you are planning your first international trip or adding a new stamp to a well-worn passport, 2026 is shaping up to be one of the most exciting years for global travel. New flight routes, improved infrastructure, and a wave of post-pandemic wanderlust have made the world more accessible than ever.

We've spoken to our network of travel experts, local guides, and seasoned travellers to compile the definitive list of the 25 best places to visit in 2026 — ranked by experience quality, value for money, and how likely you are to return home truly transformed.

Top 10 Must-Visit Destinations in 2026

01

Japan — Tokyo & Kyoto

Cherry blossoms, ancient shrines, neon-lit streets, and Michelin-starred ramen. Japan delivers sensory overload in the best possible way.

From $1,200 · Best: Mar–May
02

Switzerland — Alps & Lakes

Glacier-carved valleys, chocolate-box villages, and alpine railways that feel like moving paintings. Switzerland never disappoints.

From $1,440 · Best: Jun–Sep
03

Paris, France

The city of light never goes out of style. Art museums, boulangeries, riverside strolls — Paris is eternal and endlessly romantic.

From $1,140 · Best: Apr–Jun
04

Bali, Indonesia

Rice terraces, temple ceremonies, world-class surf breaks, and some of the most affordable luxury on earth.

From $540 · Best: Apr–Oct
05

Egypt — Pyramids & Nile

Stand before the last surviving wonder of the ancient world. A Nile cruise at sunset is one of travel's great unrepeatable moments.

From $1,050 · Best: Oct–Apr

Hidden Gems Worth Discovering in 2026

Beyond the obvious bucket-list staples, 2026 is the year to explore destinations that are rising fast in global travel rankings — places where tourism infrastructure is improving but crowds haven't yet arrived.

06

Dubai, UAE

The world's most ambitious city keeps reinventing itself. Desert safaris, record-breaking architecture, and dining scenes that rival any global capital.

From $780 · Best: Nov–Mar
07

Italy — Rome, Florence & Amalfi

History, food, fashion, and coastline. Italy is not a country — it is an argument for why beauty matters.

From $1,400 · Best: Apr–Jun & Sep–Oct
08

Morocco — Marrakech & Sahara

Souks, riads, desert dunes, and Atlantic coastline. Morocco packs more contrast into a single country than most continents.

From $1,600 · Best: Mar–May & Sep–Nov

How to Choose the Right Destination for You

The best travel destination is the one that matches your specific energy, budget, and what you need from a journey. Here is a quick framework our travel experts use with every client:

Destination Matching Framework

  • Culture seekers → Japan, Italy, India, Morocco, Peru
  • Nature lovers → Switzerland, New Zealand, Costa Rica, Norway
  • Beach & relaxation → Maldives, Bali, Seychelles, Thailand
  • Budget travellers → Vietnam, Portugal, Mexico, Sri Lanka
  • Luxury experiences → Dubai, Maldives, Monaco, French Polynesia
  • Adventure seekers → Patagonia, Iceland, Nepal, Tanzania
  • Family travel → Japan, Spain, Australia, Canada

Best Time to Book Your 2026 Trip

The golden rule of travel planning: book your international flights 3–6 months in advance for the best fares. For peak-season destinations like Japan in cherry blossom season or Switzerland in summer, hotels fill up even earlier.
