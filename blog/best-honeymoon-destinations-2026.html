Best Honeymoon Destinations
2026

From overwater villas in the Maldives to candlelit dinners in Santorini — here are the world's most romantic destinations for your perfect honeymoon.

Your honeymoon is one of the most significant trips you will ever take. It marks the beginning of a shared story — and the destination you choose sets the tone for everything that follows. Whether you dream of waking up above a turquoise lagoon, exploring cobblestone streets hand in hand, or watching the sun melt into the horizon from a private villa, 2026 offers extraordinary options at every budget.

Choosing among the best honeymoon destinations for 2026 isn't really about finding the "most beautiful" place on a list — every destination on this page is beautiful. It's about finding the one that matches how the two of you actually want to spend your first trip as a married couple. Some couples want to do absolutely nothing but read on a beach together. Others want museums, wine, and a new neighborhood to wander every afternoon. Neither is more romantic than the other — they're just different honeymoons, and getting that match right is what makes the difference between a good trip and one you talk about for the rest of your marriage.

This guide draws on real client feedback and on-the-ground research from our romance travel team, built around one simple question for every destination: does this place make love feel like the most natural thing in the world?

Top Romantic Destinations for Honeymooners in 2026

🏝 Maldives

Overwater bungalows, bioluminescent beaches, and complete privacy. The Maldives is the ultimate honeymoon destination for a reason — it feels designed for two.

From $2,400 · 7 nights

🌅 Santorini, Greece

Cliffside infinity pools, white-washed villages, and sunsets that stop time. Santorini is Europe's most romantic island, and it earns that title every evening.

From $1,800 · 6 nights

🗼 Paris, France

The original city of love. Seine riverside walks, champagne in secret wine bars, the Eiffel Tower at golden hour. Paris never fails a honeymoon.

From $1,140 · 5 nights

🌺 Bali, Indonesia

Private jungle villas, couples' spa rituals, temple ceremonies, and rice terrace sunsets. Bali is romance made tangible — and remarkably affordable.

From $540 · 7 nights

How to Choose the Right Honeymoon Destination for You

Start with a simple exercise: picture your ideal honeymoon morning. If that image involves stepping straight off your deck into the ocean, the Maldives or Seychelles will suit you far better than a city break. If it involves croissants and a slow walk to a café, Paris or Kyoto belongs on your shortlist instead. If you can't decide between the two, that's usually a sign you want a destination that offers both — which is exactly why Santorini and Bali consistently top honeymoon lists: they blend beach, culture, and quiet in a single place.

Budget matters too, and it's worth being honest about it early. A week in the Maldives can run north of $2,400 per person once you include an overwater villa, while a similarly unforgettable week in Bali can come in under $600 per person. Neither is the "better" honeymoon — they're simply different honeymoons, and knowing your number before you start browsing hotels saves a lot of back-and-forth later.

Hidden Romantic Gems Worth Considering

Beyond the classic choices, several lesser-known destinations are quickly earning their place on the honeymoon shortlist. These offer equal romance with far fewer crowds — and often significantly better value. If you'd rather share your honeymoon photos with a place that isn't already flooded with other honeymooners' photos, this is where to start looking.

Azores, Portugal

Volcanic craters filled with emerald lakes, thermal pools beside the Atlantic, and whale-watching excursions from medieval harbours. The Azores feels like the edge of the world — in the most romantic possible way. Prices are roughly 40% lower than the European mainland, and direct flights from major hubs have multiplied in recent years.

Kyoto, Japan in Autumn

If you can synchronize your honeymoon with Japan's autumn foliage season (mid-October to mid-November), Kyoto becomes one of the most breathtaking places on earth. Red and gold maple leaves frame ancient temples. Ryokan inns serve kaiseki dinners of extraordinary delicacy. The Japanese philosophy of omotenashi — hospitality as an art form — makes every couple feel uniquely celebrated.

Seychelles

If Maldives is the world's most famous island honeymoon, Seychelles is its more characterful sibling. Granite boulders frame powder beaches. Sea turtles nest on uninhabited islands. The food — a fusion of African, French and Indian influences — is among the finest in the Indian Ocean.

"The best honeymoon is not about where you go — it is about going somewhere that lets you forget about everywhere else."

Honeymoon Planning Checklist

Even the most romantic destination can feel stressful if the logistics are an afterthought. A little planning early on means you actually get to relax once you're there — which, after all, is the entire point.

When to Book

For 2026's most in-demand honeymoon destinations — Santorini in summer, Kyoto in autumn, the Maldives during dry season — booking 6 to 9 months ahead gives you the best choice of villas and the best fares. If your travel dates are flexible, booking flights 4–6 months out and watching for shoulder-season rates can meaningfully lower the total cost without sacrificing much in weather or crowds.

What to Pack and Prepare

Beyond the obvious essentials, pack travel insurance that covers both of you under a single policy, and keep a digital and physical copy of both passports somewhere separate from the originals. If your destination involves multiple flights or boat transfers — common in the Maldives and Seychelles — build in a buffer day rather than a same-day connection, so a delay doesn't cost you a night of your honeymoon.

What to Plan & When

  • Book flights 4–6 months in advance for best fares on peak routes
  • Tell your hotel it's your honeymoon — complimentary upgrades are common
  • Pack travel insurance that covers both of you under one policy
  • Plan one "surprise" experience for your partner — a private dinner, sunset cruise, or spa session
  • Mix structured days with completely free ones — some of the best honeymoon memories are unplanned
  • Don't overplan — allow at least one full day of doing absolutely nothing
  • Bring a polaroid or film camera alongside digital — the tangible photos become treasured keepsakes

Honeymoon Destinations FAQ

What is the best honeymoon destination for 2026?

There's no single best destination — only the best fit for how you and your partner like to travel. The Maldives and Seychelles suit couples who want total seclusion; Santorini and Paris suit couples who want scenery and culture in equal measure; Bali and the Azores suit couples who want both romance and real value.

How far in advance should we book our honeymoon?

For popular destinations and peak seasons — Santorini in summer, the Maldives in dry season, Kyoto during autumn foliage — book 6 to 9 months ahead for the best villa selection and airfare. Off-peak or more flexible dates can often be booked 3–4 months out with little difference in price.

What is the most affordable romantic honeymoon destination?

Bali consistently offers the best value among top-tier honeymoon destinations, with private villas and couples' experiences available from around $540 per person for a full week. The Azores in Portugal is a strong runner-up, priced roughly 40% below comparable mainland European destinations.

Should we tell our hotel it's our honeymoon?

Yes. Most hotels — especially boutique and luxury properties — offer complimentary upgrades, welcome amenities, or small romantic touches for honeymooning couples when asked in advance. It costs nothing to mention it and is one of the easiest ways to elevate the trip.

Final Thoughts: Finding Your Honeymoon

The best honeymoon destinations for 2026 all have one thing in common: they give a couple room to actually be together, without the trip itself becoming a source of stress. Whether that means an overwater villa in the Maldives, a quiet inn in the Azores, or a week in Bali that costs a fraction of the flashier options, the "right" choice is simply the one that fits your budget, your pace, and the kind of memories the two of you want to carry into married life. Start with how you want your mornings to feel, work backward from there, and the destination tends to choose itself.
