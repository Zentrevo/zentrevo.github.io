Rajasthan Tour for Foreign Tourists

Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur, Jaisalmer — heritage stays and local guides, sequenced so you spend more time in the cities than in the car.

Rajasthan is where a lot of the imagery people associate with India actually comes from — desert forts, painted old cities, and palaces that are now hotels rather than museums. It's also a large state, and fitting the right cities together in the right order matters more here than almost anywhere else in the country. Zentrevo plans private, custom Rajasthan tours for foreign tourists, built around your dates, pace, and interests rather than a fixed circuit.

This page covers the cities we plan around most often, what a Rajasthan trip typically involves, and how to put one together.

Why Rajasthan Needs Careful Planning

Rajasthan isn't one destination — it's several very different ones stitched together, and the distances between them are real. Jaipur to Jodhpur is a full day's drive if you're not careful about routing; Jaisalmer sits further out toward the desert border. Get the order wrong, or try to fit in too many stops, and you end up spending more of the trip in the car than in the cities themselves.

That's really the core of what we do here — sequencing the route sensibly, deciding how many nights each city actually deserves, and being honest if your original wishlist doesn't fit your timeframe. A well-planned 8-day Rajasthan trip usually beats a rushed 12-day one covering more ground.

Popular Rajasthan Destinations

01

Jaipur

Rajasthan's capital and usually the starting point of a trip. The City Palace, Amber Fort, and the old city's pink-hued streets and bazaars are the highlights, along with a first real introduction to Rajasthani craft — textiles, jewelry, block printing.

02

Udaipur

Built around a lake, with the City Palace and several converted palace hotels giving it a noticeably different feel from Jaipur's fort-and-bazaar energy. Slower, more romantic in tone, and a common favorite among first-time visitors.

03

Jodhpur

The "blue city," seen properly from above at Mehrangarh Fort, which sits on a hill overlooking the old town's blue-painted houses. One of the better-preserved and more atmospheric forts in Rajasthan, with genuinely good on-site museum content.

04

Jaisalmer

The furthest out of the four, right at the edge of the Thar Desert. The fort here is a living one — people still live and work inside its walls — and it's the natural base for camel rides and overnight camp stays in the dunes.

What a Rajasthan Tour Includes

Included as Standard

  • Route planning across your chosen cities
  • Private transportation between destinations
  • Hotel bookings at your chosen category, including heritage properties where available
  • Local guides at forts, palaces, and major sites
  • Airport or railway transfers at the start and end of the trip

Arranged on request, depending on your interests: desert camp stays and camel or jeep excursions near Jaisalmer, food-focused experiences like a home-cooked Rajasthani meal or a market walk, craft workshops (block printing, textiles, miniature painting), and specialty heritage stays inside palace properties where availability allows. Your itinerary quote separates what's included from what's arranged on request, so there's no ambiguity before you confirm.

Heritage Stays and Local Guides

One of the genuine draws of Rajasthan is that many hotels are former palaces or forts, not purpose-built buildings — real architecture, real history, still standing and now open to guests. Not every property is right for every traveler or budget, so we'll talk through what's realistic and what actually fits your trip, rather than defaulting to whichever heritage property is easiest to book.

Local guides in Rajasthan tend to know their specific city in real depth — a Jaipur guide isn't necessarily the best person to walk you through Mehrangarh Fort in Jodhpur. We arrange guides city by city, which means better, more specific context at each stop rather than one guide covering the whole state at a surface level.

Culture, Food, and the Desert

Rajasthan's culture runs through everything from architecture to daily life — the forts and palaces are the obvious highlights, but the food (rich, often built around lentils and dairy given the desert climate), the textile and craft traditions, and the folk music you'll hear in courtyards and markets are just as much a part of the experience.

The desert around Jaisalmer adds something genuinely different from the rest of the state — camel treks, dune camps, and a change of pace after a few days of forts and cities. It's usually one or two nights, not the centerpiece of the trip, but it tends to be one of the most memorable stretches for first-time visitors.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days do I need for a Rajasthan tour?

Most first-time itineraries covering Jaipur, Udaipur, and Jodhpur run 7-9 days at a comfortable pace. Adding Jaisalmer typically means 10-12 days, given the extra distance involved.

Can I customize which Rajasthan cities I visit?

Yes. You don't need to cover all four main cities — some travelers focus on just Jaipur and Udaipur, while others want the full route including Jaisalmer's desert. The itinerary is built around what interests you.

Is private transportation included between Rajasthan cities?

Yes, a private vehicle and dedicated driver are standard for the whole route, not shared transfers.

Are heritage hotels included, or do I need to request them?

Heritage properties can be included as part of your itinerary where availability allows — mention your interest when planning and we'll factor it in.

Is Jaisalmer worth including if I'm short on time?

It's worth it if you have the days for it, since it's the furthest stop and adds real travel time. If your trip is under 8 days, we'll usually suggest prioritizing Jaipur and Udaipur (or Jodhpur) instead.

Can Zentrevo arrange a desert camp stay near Jaisalmer?

Yes, camel or jeep excursions and overnight desert camps can be arranged as part of your Jaisalmer stop.

How do I request a custom Rajasthan itinerary?

Use the contact form with your travel dates, the cities you're interested in, and your approximate budget, and we'll follow up with a draft itinerary to review.
