Best Digital Nomad Destinations for 2026

Fast wifi, fair visas, and a real sense of community — these are the eight places actually worth relocating your laptop to this year.

Remote work stopped being a novelty years ago, and by 2026 the infrastructure has finally caught up. More than 60 countries now offer a dedicated digital nomad visa, and the destinations that do it well have built entire micro-economies around coworking spaces, long-stay apartments, and communities that actually welcome remote workers rather than merely tolerating them.

We surveyed our network of relocated clients and remote-work partners to rank the destinations delivering the best combination of connectivity, cost, visa access, and quality of life this year.

Top Digital Nomad Destinations for 2026

01

Lisbon, Portugal

Portugal's D8 digital nomad visa remains one of Europe's most accessible, and Lisbon pairs Atlantic light with a genuinely international coworking scene.

From $1,300/mo · Visa: D8
02

Canggu & Ubud, Bali

Still the reigning capital of remote work culture, with the fastest fibre internet in Southeast Asia and a five-year second-home visa option.

From $900/mo · Visa: B211A / KITAS
03

Dubai, UAE

Zero income tax, world-class infrastructure, and a one-year remote work visa make Dubai the top pick for higher-earning nomads.

From $2,200/mo · Visa: Virtual Working Programme
04

Mexico City, Mexico

Same time zone as most of the US, incredible food, and a temporary resident visa that's genuinely straightforward to renew.

From $1,100/mo · Visa: Temporary Resident

What Actually Makes a Good Nomad Base

Wifi speed gets all the attention, but our clients consistently rank three other factors higher once they've actually relocated: reliable healthcare access, a manageable visa renewal process, and — perhaps most underrated — a walkable neighbourhood with a real local community, not just an expat bubble.

Before You Book a One-Way Ticket

  • Check the destination's minimum income requirement for its nomad visa before applying
  • Buy international health insurance that explicitly covers remote workers, not just tourists
  • Test your setup with a 2-week trial stay before committing to 3+ months
  • Join local coworking Slack or Discord communities before you arrive
  • Keep copies of tax residency documents — many nomads unintentionally trigger dual tax obligations (the IRS international taxpayers guidance is a useful starting point for US citizens)

"The best nomad destinations in 2026 aren't the cheapest — they're the ones where you can actually build a routine."

Visa Rules Are Tightening — Plan Ahead

Several popular nomad hubs introduced stricter minimum-stay and minimum-income thresholds in the past year as governments look to formalize what was previously a grey-area tourist visa workaround. It's worth checking each country's official immigration portal directly before booking flights, since requirements can shift with little notice.

Our Zentrevo relocation team can help handle visa paperwork, long-stay apartment sourcing, and local SIM/insurance setup for any of the destinations above — it's one of the more popular add-ons we've introduced this year for clients transitioning to remote-first travel.

Before finalizing any move, check entry and residency requirements directly against the U.S. State Department's travel advisories and the UN Tourism (UNWTO) for the latest country-level guidance, since visa rules for remote workers continue to shift year to year.
