Best Road Trip Routes 2026

From California's Pacific coastline to Scotland's rugged Highlands — the routes worth putting real miles on this year.

There's a particular kind of freedom in a good road trip that no flight itinerary can replicate — the ability to just pull over when the view demands it. 2026's best routes combine genuinely dramatic scenery with infrastructure reliable enough that you're not gambling on fuel stops or cell signal.

Top Road Trip Routes for 2026

01

Pacific Coast Highway, California

Big Sur's cliffside curves remain one of the most photographed stretches of road anywhere, with Hearst Castle and Monterey as natural stopovers.

5-7 days · Best: Apr–Jun & Sep–Oct
02

North Coast 500, Scotland

A 516-mile loop through the Highlands — castles, single-track roads, and coastline that rivals Norway's fjords on a good weather day.

6-9 days · Best: May–Sep
03

Ring Road, Iceland

828 miles circling the entire island, passing waterfalls, glaciers, and black sand beaches roughly every 40 minutes of driving.

7-10 days · Best: Jun–Aug
04

Great Ocean Road, Australia

The Twelve Apostles rock formations anchor this Victoria coastline drive, with rainforest and surf towns along the way.

3-5 days · Best: Dec–Feb

Rental Car Logistics Worth Knowing

Check the U.S. State Department travel advisories for road conditions and safety before departure. International driving permits are required alongside your regular license in several countries on this list — check requirements well before departure, since some rental companies will refuse a car without one. Manual transmission remains standard in the UK and much of Europe; confirm automatic availability specifically if that matters to you, as it often costs more and books out faster.

Road Trip Planning Essentials

  • Check the official government travel advisory for your route's countries before departure
  • Download offline maps — several of these routes pass through genuine cell dead zones
  • Book accommodation ahead in peak season; small towns along scenic routes have limited rooms
  • Factor in fuel stops carefully on remote stretches like Iceland's Ring Road or the NC500
  • Purchase rental insurance that covers single-track and unpaved roads if your route includes them

"The best road trip photo is never the one you planned. It's the pull-off you almost didn't take."

Building in Slack Time

The single biggest planning mistake on scenic road trips is under-budgeting time — treating the drive as transit rather than the destination itself. Add at least 30-40% more time than a maps app estimates, since these routes are built for stopping, not through-driving.
