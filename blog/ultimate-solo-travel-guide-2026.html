The Ultimate Solo Travel Guide for 2026

Safe destinations, smart packing, and why traveling alone is one of the most transformative things you can do.

Solo travel isn't a compromise made when you can't find a travel companion — for a growing number of people, it's the preferred way to travel. No compromise on itinerary, an easier path to genuine local encounters, and a kind of independence that's hard to replicate in group travel.

Best Solo Travel Destinations for 2026

01

Japan

Exceptional public transport, low crime, and a culture where solo dining and solo travel are fully normalized — an ideal first solo destination.

Best: Mar–May & Oct–Nov
02

Portugal

Affordable, walkable, and an established hostel and coworking scene that makes meeting other travelers genuinely easy.

Best: Apr–Jun & Sep
03

New Zealand

A strong backpacker culture and outdoor adventure infrastructure built specifically around solo and small-group travelers.

Best: Dec–Feb

Packing for One

Solo travelers carry their own bags for the entire trip, which makes packing light more than a nicety — it's practical necessity. A carry-on-only approach, one pair of versatile shoes, and layering rather than bulk tends to work better on solo trips than on group trips where luggage gets distributed across a rental car.

Solo Travel Essentials

  • Book the first night's accommodation before you land, even if you plan to wing the rest
  • Download offline maps for your destination before departure
  • Join hostel-organized activities or walking tours — the easiest way to meet people in the first 48 hours
  • Keep a digital and physical copy of your passport, stored separately from the original, and consider registering your trip with the Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP) if you're a U.S. citizen
  • Check your accommodation's cancellation policy in case plans shift, which they often do solo

"Traveling alone doesn't mean being alone. It just means nobody else's schedule gets a vote."

The Adjustment Period Is Real

Most first-time solo travelers report an adjustment period of 2–3 days before it starts to feel comfortable rather than isolating. That's normal, not a sign the trip isn't working. It usually passes once the first few genuine local or fellow-traveler interactions happen.

Zentrevo's solo travel packages include pre-vetted, solo-friendly accommodation and can connect first-time solo travelers with group activities and day tours at each stop — a useful middle ground between fully independent and fully guided travel.

Before departure, check your government's official travel advisory — the U.S. State Department travel advisories and GOV.UK foreign travel advice are both reliable, regularly updated sources for entry and safety guidance.
