Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning and booking a trip with us.

How does Zentrevo plan my trip?
You share your destination ideas, dates, and budget with us via the contact form. Our team then builds a personalized itinerary and sends you a proposal to review before anything is booked.
How far in advance should I book?
For most international trips, we recommend starting the planning process 2–4 months ahead. Peak season travel — holidays, popular safari or ski windows — often benefits from 6+ months of lead time.
Can I make changes after booking?
Yes, though change policies depend on the specific hotel, airline, or operator involved. We'll always let you know what's flexible and what isn't before you confirm a booking.
Do you handle visas and travel documents?
We can guide you on visa and documentation requirements for your destination, but you are responsible for obtaining valid passports, visas, and any required permits. See our destination guides on the blog for specific requirements.
Is travel insurance included?
Travel insurance is not automatically included in our packages. We strongly recommend purchasing appropriate coverage, particularly for adventure travel, cruises, or high-altitude trekking.
What payment methods do you accept?
Payment details and accepted methods are shared directly during the booking process, once your itinerary and pricing are finalized.
How do I get in touch if I need help during my trip?
Our team is reachable at hello@zentrevo.com for any pre-trip or during-trip questions.
