FAQ
Common Questions
How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend starting the conversation 6–8 weeks ahead for international trips, and around 4 weeks for domestic ones. That said, we can often accommodate shorter timelines — just get in touch and we'll let you know what's realistic.
Is travel insurance included? +
Travel insurance isn't automatically bundled into every package, but we can point you toward suitable coverage for your trip and destination — just ask when we're planning your itinerary.
Can I customise a package? +
Absolutely. Every itinerary can be tailored — different hotels, extra nights, added experiences, solo vs. group travel. Just tell us what you want and we'll build around it.
What payment options are available? +
Payment methods depend on your destination and booking, and we'll walk you through exactly what's accepted once your itinerary is finalized — we work with travelers worldwide, so options vary by region.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Cancellation terms depend on the specific hotels, airlines, and operators in your itinerary, since each sets its own policy. We'll always explain the exact terms clearly before you confirm a booking.
