Visa Assistance

Work Visa

For overseas employment and long-term work assignments.

What This Visa Is For

For overseas employment and long-term work assignments. At Zentrevo, work visa applications are handled as a complete service rather than a checklist you're left to manage alone — from the very first document to the final approval, our visa specialists stay involved at every step.

The Application Process

The process typically starts with a short consultation to understand your travel dates, purpose of visit and destination-specific requirements for the work visa. From there, we prepare a document checklist tailored to your situation, review everything before submission to catch issues that commonly cause delays or rejections, and handle the appointment scheduling or online submission on your behalf wherever the process allows it. Every application is different, and we treat it that way rather than running everyone through an identical template.

Timelines & What To Expect

Processing times vary by country and visa category, and can also shift with seasonal demand — something our team tracks closely so you're not caught off guard close to your travel date. Once your work visa application is submitted, we keep you updated on its status rather than leaving you to check portals or make embassy calls yourself, and we flag early if any additional documentation is requested, so there's time to respond without it becoming a last-minute scramble.

Common Mistakes We Catch

A surprising number of visa applications run into avoidable delays: incomplete financial documents, inconsistent travel dates across paperwork, photos that don't meet spec, or forms filled out slightly incorrectly. Because we handle work visa applications regularly, we catch these issues before submission rather than after a rejection — which is usually the difference between a smooth approval and a frustrating back-and-forth that costs both time and money.

Planning Around Your Travel Dates

Handling a work visa application on your own is possible, but it usually means learning an unfamiliar process under time pressure, often right when you're also trying to plan flights and accommodation. Working with Zentrevo means that visa piece is off your plate entirely — we've done this hundreds of times, we know what each embassy or consulate typically asks for, and we catch the small mistakes that trip up first-time applicants before they cause a delay.

Why Use Zentrevo vs. DIY

It's also worth planning your work visa timeline around more than just the processing window — factoring in passport validity requirements, any supporting appointments that may be needed, and enough buffer before your travel date that an unexpected request for additional documents doesn't put your trip at risk. This is exactly the kind of detail our team tracks on your behalf, so a work visa application never becomes the reason a trip gets delayed.

After Approval & Next Steps

Once your work visa is approved, our team doesn't simply disappear — we can help align your visa validity with your actual travel plans, flag any conditions attached to the visa (such as permitted length of stay or number of entries), and make sure you're clear on what to carry with you when you travel.
